The bar already has a profile slot — today it reads Default. It becomes the role, because the role is the single most consequential thing about the screen an operator is looking at, and a supervisor walking up needs to read it without touching anything.

Where the role is chosen

Runtime library — each app card offers its profiles by name, so launching is choosing. This also answers finding 9: remembered role entry. The last choice on this device is preselected, never auto-launched.

Maintenance → Settings — changing role mid-session needs the 1.5 s hold, because it is not an operating control.

What the role may change

Layout, target size, how a limit is expressed, whether details are visible, scan/dwell timing, language.

And nothing else.

What it must never change

Topics, message types, command frame, dead zone semantics, publish rate, STOP behaviour, policy allowlists. Bench and Operator send byte-identical messages. If a role could change what reaches the manager, a bench test would stop being evidence about the operator's session.

1c Drive · Operator Drive · Operator

The person the arm is for. One reach arc on the right; the left column is read, not touched. Speed limits are three 64 px targets instead of a continuous slider, because a slider demands the exact fine motor control this product exists to stop demanding.
