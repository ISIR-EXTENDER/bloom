3c Two findings that need your call
Finding · Robot feedback and Command sources do not fit

Robot feedback declares six widgets: a gauge, three plots and two echoes. At the legal minimums — plot 280×180, echo 226×200 — plus the bench rail and STOP, the stage holds four. Command sources declares six too, and holds four.

Today they fit only because the echoes are 336×380 with 15 px type and the plots are 400×250 traces nobody can read a 30-second history from. It is the same over-subscription as kinova's Drive, one screen further along.

The options are: split each into two screens; move the joint-state and jacobian views to Bloom Debug where lilac surfaces are allowed; or make the plots on-demand — one large plot with a topic picker instead of three small ones. I'd take the third for Sources and the second for Feedback, but which diagnostics your team actually reads mid-session is not something I can infer from the seed.

Resolved · keep them separate — Susi, 17 Sep

Once both sit on the same column grid, 3b is 1b plus four frame buttons and a twist echo, minus the two speed limits. Same pads, same sliders, same gripper, same rail, same STOP.

They stay two screens, and the reason reframes the split: Operator is the accessible layout — for people with motor disabilities — and it differs from Bench in language as much as geometry. Speed as Slow / Medium / Fast rather than 0.15 m/s; buttons named for what they do rather than what they publish.

Bench is for debugging, used mostly by people without motor impairment, so it may carry more information and smaller targets than the operator surface — 48 px tablet density rather than 64, detail strips where geometry allows. What this changes in the contract: a role now varies wording too, so plain-language copy becomes a property of the operator profile. Topics, frames, dead zones, publish rate and STOP behaviour stay identical — the two still send byte-identical messages.
